Not for navigation. The underlay is a reference basemap, not a hydrographic chart. Esri state that GEBCO bathymetry must not be used for navigation or any purpose relating to safety at sea, and global resolution outside US waters only reaches about 1:577,000. Satellite imagery shows reef platforms well but carries no soundings and no dependable capture date.

Bar entrances. Marked in lilac. A bar block is downgraded whenever the tide is ebbing with real swell running, because that is when the bar stands up. This is a prompt to look, not a clearance. Get eyes on the bar, check VMR or Coast Guard, and cross on the flood if you have the choice.

Tidal flow. Derived from how fast modelled sea level is changing, not from a tidal stream atlas. Timing of the turn will be about right, speed is indicative only. The marine model runs at 8 km and does not resolve the channels or the bar itself.

Wind grid spacing does not capture reef-scale sheltering. The lee will always fish better than the number shown. Cross-check the relevant BoM coastal waters forecast before you leave.
