Your thresholds
Not for navigation. The underlay is a reference basemap, not a hydrographic chart. Esri state that GEBCO bathymetry must not be used for navigation or any purpose relating to safety at sea, and global resolution outside US waters only reaches about 1:577,000. Satellite imagery shows reef platforms well but carries no soundings and no dates you can rely on. Use your plotter and paper charts for anything you steer by.

On the tidal flow column. Derived from how fast modelled sea level is changing, not from a tidal stream atlas. Timing of the turn will be about right, speed is indicative only. The marine model runs at 8 km and does not resolve the channels you actually dive.

Wind grid spacing does not capture reef-scale sheltering. The lee will always fish better than the number shown. Cross-check the BoM Capricornia coastal waters forecast before you leave.
