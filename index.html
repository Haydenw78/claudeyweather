Swell trains
The single sea figure is an average of everything running, which can mislead: a reported 1.8 m at 11 s might really be 1.6 m at 8 s from the east with 0.8 m at 14 s from the south underneath. Arrows show which way each train is travelling. Bearings are given the standard way, as the direction the swell comes from.
Model agreement
A different question from the ensemble band: do independent forecasting centres agree? Where the lines braid together you can plan on it. Where they fan apart the pattern is unresolved, and a narrow ensemble will not save you.

Dive log & visibility calibration
The visibility model runs on educated guesses. Log what you actually saw and it captures every driver the app believed at that moment, so the coefficients can be fitted properly later rather than argued about.
Backfill a past dive
Every driver is reconstructable, so a date and a remembered number is enough. Works back to 1940 for wind and to 2022 for waves. Good for old trips, dive shop reports, or anything your groups have posted with a date on it.
Or paste a batch
One per line: a date, a number, and optionally a time or the word "high". A bare line naming a spot switches which spot the following lines belong to.

Not for navigation. The underlay is a reference basemap, not a hydrographic chart. Esri state that GEBCO bathymetry must not be used for navigation or any purpose relating to safety at sea.

Past and previous runs. The past track is model analysis, the closest thing to what actually happened, not a buoy reading. Run −1d and Run −3d show what the same model was predicting for these same hours one and three days ago. If successive runs keep shifting one way, the model is still hunting and the current run is unlikely to be the last word.

Slack water. From turning points in modelled sea level, not a tidal stream atlas. Times should be close, speed is indicative. Which way the stream sets is not in this data. Flooding and ebbing mean rising and falling water, nothing about heading. Drift is large-scale ocean current and a separate thing again.

Seasonal outlook. ERA5 reads windier than a point forecast, so use the percentile framing. ECMWF EC46 is not bias-corrected and is area guidance.
